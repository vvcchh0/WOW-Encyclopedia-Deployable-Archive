WORLD OF WARCRAFT NORTH AMERICAN ARENA TOURNAMENT

OFFICIAL COMPETITION RULES

NO PURCHASE NECESSARY FOR CANADIAN RESIDENTS.

Note that characters that are playable on servers or "Realms" that Sponsor adds to the list of World of Warcraft Realms after the Commencement Date (defined below) are not eligible for this Contest. If you are under the legal age of majority in your country of residence, but over the age of 13 (a "Minor"), you may still enter and be one of the Eligible Participants if you otherwise meet all of the eligibility criteria set forth above and one of your parents or legal guardians read and agree to these Tournament rules on your behalf in order to participate in the Regional Tournaments or the Finals, and win be eligible to win a prize. All applicable federal, state and local laws and regulations apply. Eligible Participants must have a World of Warcraft account which includes an associated valid email address. Sponsor reserves the right to verify the email address provided to Sponsor by the Eligible Participant. The Eligible Participant must be the authorized account holder of the Account. "Authorized account holder" is defined as the natural person who registered the Account on the World of Warcraft gaming service. There is a limit of one Arena Team per Eligible Participant per Qualification Round.

  3. Tournament Period.

    The Tournament shall commence on the start of the First Qualification Round of the Tournament, which shall be at 10:00 AM Pacific Time (5:00 PM GMT) on March 31, 2008 (the "Commencement Date"), and shall continue through the end of the Arena Tournament Finals, which is expected to conclude on or about October 2008.

  4. Entry; Entry Fee.

    To enter, simply go to the Account Management page of www.worldofwarcraft.com, log in, using your account name and password, click on the link for the Tournament, and pay the applicable entry fee. The Entry Fee for the Tournament is $20.00 USD for the First Qualification Round of the Tournament, and $20.00 USD for the Second Qualification Round of the Tournament. Canadian residents are not required to pay an Entry Fee in order to enter. Instead, Canadian residents may enter by submitting a 250 word typewritten essay comparing the video gaming culture in Canada to the video gaming culture in the United States on 8 ½ x 11 inch paper and mailing their essay to Essay Entry for The North American Blizzard Entertainment Arena Tournament, P.O Box 18979, Irvine, CA 92623. Essay entries must be received no later than March 31, 2008 in order to be eligible. Essay entrants represent and warrant that the essay is their original work and does not infringe the rights of any third party. By entering, essay entrants hereby grant, without further consideration, all right, title and interest in and to their essay to Sponsor.

  5. Arena Teams, and Participation in the Tournament

    1. Tournament Realms. The Tournament shall take place on one or more realms which are activated specifically for Eligible Participants (the "Tournament Realms"). Each World of Warcraft character that an Eligible Participant creates on this realm is a level 70 Character, with high level armor, weapons, trinkets, and other items, and all talent points available for talent specialization.

    2. Arena Teams. The Tournament shall determine the top "Arena Team" made up of no less than three (3), and no more than six (6) Characters played by Eligible Participants who are of the same "faction," i.e., an Arena Team must be made up of all "Alliance" Characters, or all "Horde" Characters. The members of an Arena Team ("Team Members") shall be designated by one (1) Eligible Participant for each Arena Team who operates as the "Team Captain," and who purchases an Arena Team charter from an Arena Promoter using World of Warcraft Gold provided to their Character, and then invites other Eligible Players to join the Arena Team. All Arena Team members must be Eligible Participants and must separately enter in order to participate. Competition in the Tournament shall take place in the Arena Battlegrounds in World of Warcraft, and in accordance with the World of Warcraft Terms of Use and Arena Battleground rules. To engage in an Arena match an Arena team will 'queue' or 'sign up' to enter an Arena Battleground with an "Arena Battlemaster." Once an Arena Team is queued, the Arena Battleground system will match the Arena Team up with another Arena Team, and the match will take place. The match will then commence, and continues until only one Arena Team is left. The Arena Team with players alive at the end of the match is the winner. If an Arena Team competes in ten (10) Arena Matches over a week long time period, then that Arena Team receives a "rating" based on its performance in those ten (10) Arena Matches in accordance with the standard rating system for Arena tournament matches (an "Arena Rating") available at http://www.worldofwarcraft.com/pvp/arena/index.xml. Arena Teams may compete in as many matches as it is able to participate in during the First Qualification Round of the Tournament. For more information on the Arena Battlegrounds, and the rules that govern combat in the Arena Battlegrounds, please go to http://www.worldofwarcraft.com/pvp/arena/index.xml/

    3. Team Participation in the Tournament. During the First and, if applicable, Second Qualification Rounds of the Tournament (described below), an Arena Team may utilize any three (3) Characters who are Team Members of the Arena Team to make up a three member team to compete in the Arena. In the event that an Arena Team qualifies for the Regional Qualifier (described below), the Team Captain must designate three (3) Team Members from the Arena Team who will be eligible to compete in the Regional Qualifier, and if the Arena Team qualifies, for the Finals. In the event one or more Team Members are unable (due to illness, absence or other reasons beyond their control) to participate in the First or Second Qualification Rounds, the Team Captain may substitute another Team Member, if available.

  6. Tournament Rules.

    All matches that take place in the Tournament shall take place in accordance with the World of Warcraft Arena Rules, which are available at http://www.worldofwarcraft.com/pvp/arena/index.xml. In the event of any conflict between these Official Rules and the World of Warcraft Arena Rules, these Official Rules shall prevail. "Cheating," as determined in Sponsor's sole discretion, and specifically including, without limitation, "win-trading," shall result in disqualification of the Arena Team and all of its Team Members. Third party user interfaces compatible with World of Warcraft, and which do not violate the World of Warcraft Terms of Use may be used during the First and, if applicable, Second Qualification Rounds of the Tournament, but will not be allowed at the Regional Qualifier or the Finals. At the Regional Qualifier, and the Finals, Team Members will have fifteen (15) minutes prior to the start of the first match with each opponent Team Member to prepare the computer on which they will use to participate in the Tournament match.

  7. Determination of Winners.

    1. Qualification Rounds and Regional Qualifier Tournaments

      1. The First Qualification Round of the Tournament. The First Qualification Round of the Tournament shall last for approximately six weeks, and will commence on March 31, 2008 at approximately 10:00 AM Pacific Time (5:00 PM GMT) (subject to 'Server Maintenance' to be performed by Sponsor), and end on May 20, 2008 at 2:00 AM Pacific Time ( 9:00 AM GMT). The first two weeks of the First Qualification Round will be a "practice" period, and during this time Eligible Participants may switch from one team to another without that Arena Team being penalized. After the "Practice Period," all Arena Rating points will be set to 1,500, and Arena Teams shall compete to earn an Arena Rating based on their participation in 3 vs. 3 matches held in the "Arena Player vs. Player Battlegrounds" on the Tournament Servers. During the two weeks immediately following the end of the Practice Period, if a player switches from one Arena Team to another, the Arena Team that the Player switches to will have its Arena Rating penalized by subtracting 150 points from its Arena Rating. During the last two weeks of the First Qualification Round of the Tournament, players cannot switch Arena Teams. To qualify for the Regional Qualifier Tournament, each Arena Team must play a minimum of 200 games. In order for a Team Member on the qualifying Arena Team to be eligible for the Regional Qualifier Tournament, the Team Member must participate in at least 30% of the Arena Team's matches played during the First Qualification Round. At the end of the First Qualification Round of the Tournament, the four Arena Teams who have the highest Arena Rating will receive invitations to compete in the Regional Qualifier Tournament. In the event of a "tie" where more than one Arena Team is tied for the last position eligible for an invitation to the Regional Qualifier Tournament, a single elimination "mini tournament" will be held between the Arena Teams tied for that last position, and the winner of the "mini tournament" will be invited to the compete in the Regional Finals. The date, time and brackets for such a "mini tournament shall be determined by Sponsor, in its sole discretion. Note that the teams from the First Qualification Round of the Tournament that qualify for the Regional Qualifier Tournament may participate in the Practice Period of the Second Qualification Round of the Tournament without the necessity of paying an additional Entry Fee (or, if Canadian, writing an additional essay), but they may not compete in ranked games which take place during the Second Qualification Round of the Tournament after the Practice Period is over.

      2. The Second Qualification Round of the Tournament. The Second Qualification Round of the Tournament shall commence on June 3, 2008 at approximately 11:00 AM Pacific Standard Time ( 6:00 PM GMT) (subject to 'Server Maintenance' to be performed by Sponsor) and end on July 15, 2008 at 2:00 AM, Pacific Standard Time (9:00 AM GMT). The first two weeks of the Second Qualification Round will be a "practice" period, and during this time Eligible Participants may switch from one team to another without that Arena Team being penalized. After the "Practice Period," all Arena Rating points will be set to 1,500, and Arena Teams shall compete to earn an Arena Rating based on their participation in 3 vs. 3 matches held in the "Arena Player vs. Player Battlegrounds" on the Tournament Servers. During the two weeks immediately following the end of the Practice Period, if a player switches from one Arena Team to another, the Arena Team that the Player switches to will have its Arena Rating penalized by subtracting 150 points from its Arena Rating. During the last two weeks of the Second Qualification Round of the Tournament, players cannot switch Arena Teams. To qualify for the Regional Qualifier Tournament, each Arena Team must play a minimum of 200 games. In order for a Team Member on the qualifying Arena Team to be eligible for the Regional Qualifier Tournament, the Team Member must participate in at least 30% of the Arena Team's matches played during the First Qualification Round. At the end of the Second Qualification Round of the Tournament, the four Arena Teams who have the highest Arena Rating will receive invitations to compete in their respective Regional Qualifier Tournament, as described below. In the event of a "tie" where more than one Arena Team is tied for the last position eligible for an invitation to the Regional Qualifier Tournament, a single elimination "mini tournament" will be held between the Arena Teams tied for that last position, and the winner of the "mini tournament" will be invited to the compete in the Regional Finals. The date, time and brackets for such a "mini tournament shall be determined by Sponsor, in its sole discretion.

      3. The Regional Qualifier Tournament. The dates, times and locations for the Regional Qualifier Tournament will be determined by Sponsor in its sole discretion, and shall be distributed to the Team Members of the Arena Teams that qualify for the Regional Qualifier Tournament by e-mail no less than two weeks before the dates of their respective Regional Qualifier Tournament. The Eligible Participants who qualify for the Regional Qualifier Tournament will compete in a "best 3 out 5, double elimination" format to determine who will qualify for the Finals. At the Regional Qualifier, Arena Matches will be limited to a time period of twenty (20) minutes. If at the expiration of the time limit there is no clear winner, as determined by Sponsor in its sole discretion, the two Arena Teams shall play in another Arena Match to determine the winner, and if at the end of twenty (20) minutes there is no clear winner in the second match, as determined by Sponsor in its sole discretion, the winning Arena Team will be determined based on which Arena Team did the most damage to its opponent during the initial match.

  8. Prizes.

    1. Regional Qualifier Tournament.
      1. Participation in the Regional Tournament: Up to three Team Members of each Arena Team which qualify to compete at a Regional Qualifier Tournament will win a trip to the Regional Qualifier Tournament in their Region that includes: (i) round trip coach air fare from the regional airport nearest the Team Member's residence to the site of the Regional Qualifier Tournament; and (ii) two (2) nights lodging (double occupancy) at a hotel of Sponsor's choice, meals and ground transportation, as determined by Sponsor, in its sole discretion. The approximate retail price of this prize package is estimated at $2,000.00 U.S., depending upon the cost of travel for the Eligible Participant to attend the Regional Qualifier Tournament. All travel must take place on the dates and times as specified by Sponsor. If one or more Eligible Participant(s) who are part of an Arena Team cannot attend the Regional Qualifier Tournament, such Arena Team will still participate, but without such Team Member(s). Eligible Participants who qualify to compete in the Regional Tournament shall be responsible for obtaining, at their own cost and expense, all the necessary visas, passports, or other travel documents issued by the government of their country of citizenship so that they have the legal capacity to travel to the United States of America. Winners are responsible for any and all other costs and expenses associated with their prize that are not specifically set forth herein. In the event that an Eligible Team Member from an Arena Team that qualifies for a Regional Qualifier Tournament is a "minor" in their country of residence, that Team Member must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian, who shall be responsible for all of their own travel costs.
      2. Fifth through Eighth Place Winners. The Eligible Participants that compete on behalf of the Arena Team that competes in the Regional Qualifier Tournament but that do not qualify as First, Second or Third place winners shall receive a prize package consisting of a check in the amount of two hundred US dollars ($200.00 USD).
      3. Fourth Place Winner. The Eligible Participants that compete on behalf of the Arena Team that places third in the Regional Qualifier Tournament shall receive a prize package consisting of a check in the amount of five hundred US dollars ($500.00 USD).
      4. Third Place Winner. The Eligible Participants that compete on behalf of the Arena Team that places third in the Regional Qualifier Tournament shall receive a prize package consisting of a check in the amount of one thousand US dollars ($1,000.00 USD).
      5. Second Place Winner. The Eligible Participants that compete on behalf of the Arena Team that places second in the Regional Qualifier Tournament shall receive a prize package consisting of a check in the amount of two thousand US dollars ($2,000.00 USD).
      6. First Place Winner. The Eligible Participants that compete on behalf of the Arena Team that places first in the Regional Qualifier Tournament shall receive a prize package consisting of a check in the amount of five thousand US dollars ($5,000.00 USD).

    2. Invitation to Compete at the 2008 World of Warcraft Global Invitational Arena Championships. In addition to the prizes referenced above, up to three players to be designated by their Arena Team Captain from the First Place Winner and the Second Place Winner who have played in the Regional Qualifier will receive an invitation to the 2008 World of Warcraft Global Invitational World Championships, to be held in October, 2008 at a location to be designated by Sponsor. The Eligible Participants for the Arena Teams that are invited to compete in the Finals shall have seven (7) days to communicate their acceptance of their invitation, or Sponsor may (but is not required to) choose the next most eligible Arena Team to compete based on a Team's final standing in the Arena Team's respective Qualifier Tournament. Those Eligible Participants who are members of the Teams invited to the 2008 World of Warcraft Global Invitational World Championships will each win: (i) round trip coach air fare from the regional airport nearest the Team Member's residence to the site of the 2008 World of Warcraft Global Invitational World Championships (Sponsor may substitute ground transportation for airfare, in its sole discretion, if the winner(s) are within driving distance to such site); and (ii) two (2) nights lodging (double occupancy) at a hotel of Sponsor's choice, meals and ground transportation, as determined by Sponsor, in its sole discretion. The approximate retail price of this prize package is estimated at $2,000.00 U.S., depending upon the cost of travel for the Eligible Participant to attend the 2008 World of Warcraft Global Invitational World Championships. All travel must take place on the dates and times as specified by Sponsor. If one or more Eligible Participant(s) who are part of an Arena Team cannot attend the 2008 World of Warcraft Global Invitational Arena Championships, such Arena Team will still participate, but without such Team Member(s). Eligible Participants who qualify to compete in the 2008 World of Warcraft Global Invitational Arena Championships shall be responsible, at their own cost and expense, for obtaining all the necessary visas, passports, or other travel documents issued by the government of their country of citizenship so that they have the legal capacity to travel to the United States of America. Winners are responsible for any and all other costs and expenses associated with their prize that are not specifically set forth herein. In the event that an Eligible Team Member from an Arena Team that qualifies for a 2008 World of Warcraft Global Invitational Arena Championships is a "minor" in their country of residence, that Team Member must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian, traveling on the same itinerary, and who shall be responsible for all of their own travel and accommodation costs including, without limitation, transportation and accommodation costs and any cost or expense associated with obtaining all the necessary visas, passports, or other travel documents issued by the government of their country of citizenship so that they have the legal capacity to travel to the United States of America.
