2008 Promotion Code Retrieval — Historical Exhibit
Language:

ehind the scenes, Blizzard's marketing gnomes and business development goblins are working tirelessly to make sure that all your desires for Warcraft-related merchandise are met and no opportunity for promoting World of Warcraft is missed. You can see the latest, and quite possibly most exciting result of their efforts here on this page: the promotion code program.

Every once in a while, you will have the chance to receive a promotion code from one of our World of Warcraft promos. For example, the World of Warcraft Trading Card Game from Upper Deck includes rare loot cards that will provide you with promotion codes for some cool looking or just plain funny items for your World of Warcraft characters. The BlizzCon promotion codes are another example of this type of promo. If you have a promotion code, the 2008 service could generate an in-game code for a reward; that service is retired.

The 2008 instructions described selecting a region and realm, entering a promotion code and security input, and receiving an in-game code; the service is retired.

To retrieve your item, log into the game and travel to Booty Bay in Stranglethorn Vale. There, you will need to find a goblin by the name of Landro Longshot. He usually hangs out by the docks. Try asking other players in town if you're having trouble finding him. When you find Landro, he'll ask you which item your code is for; check your loot card if you're not sure which item that is. Select the item from Landro's list of available promotions, and then enter the in-game code you wrote down (you did write it down, didn't you?). If your code checks out, then Landro will hand you your reward. Congratulations - you are now the coolest player in town!

If you are redeeming a BlizzCon murloc costume or pet, please follow the instructions on the next page to find your murloc adoption representative in either Orgrimmar or Ironforge.

Note: You will only receive one promotion item, and it will be given only to the character that you choose to claim it with in-game. Be sure you choose the right character!

Promotion Retrieval Center
Important Note: Please be sure you have at least one inventory space available when redeeming promotional items from the appropriate NPC. Failure to do so will result in the loss of the item.
