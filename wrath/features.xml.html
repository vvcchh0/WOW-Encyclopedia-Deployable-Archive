Dragonblight

Date Added:  3.13.08

For millennia the Great Dragonblight has served as the enduring heart of Northrend. Here dense forests surround a vast arctic wasteland littered with the massive, gleaming bones of perished dragons.

Grizzly Hills

Date Added:  2.14.08

The lush, primordial mountains of the Grizzly Hills are a stark contrast to the snowy peaks that separate it from the Howling Fjord to the south.

Borean Tundra

Date Added:  7.29.07

The sprawling Borean Tundra lies at the far southwestern tip of Northrend, the icy continent also known as the roof of the world.

Howling Fjord

Date Added:  7.29.07

The Howling Fjord sits high above the Great Sea on the southeastern tip of Northrend. It is said that in ancient times the vrykul, a race of half-giant warriors, inhabited the land, founding a vast and prosperous civilization.
