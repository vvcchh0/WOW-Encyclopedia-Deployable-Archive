World of Warcraft Atlas Gift Pack now available! - Blaze on 2/28/06

The World of Warcraft Atlas Gift Pack from BradyGames is now available for purchase in the Blizzard Online Store. The Atlas is a comprehensive hardcover resource that includes maps of all regions and major cities as well as detailed plans of places such as Everlook and Ghostwalker Post, revealing critical locations and characters. Shrink-wrapped within this Gift Pack come eight exclusive full-color bookmarks, one for each race in World of Warcraft: Human, Night Elf, Dwarf, Gnome, Orc, Troll, Undead, and Tauren. Each bookmark includes a character image on the front and has the race description and coat of arms on the back. Read more about it here.

Richard Knaak at the NY Comic-Con - Frost on 2/24/06

This weekend, the first edition of the New York Comic-Con will take place February 24-26 at the Jacob Javits Center in New York, NY. Attending the convention will be none other than Richard Knaak, author of many Warcraft and Diablo novels. Richard will be at the convention to present his latest projects and to spend some time with his readers and everyone else who is interested in his contributions to the rich Warcraft and Diablo universes. Get an in-depth look at Shadows of Ice, the next installment in the Sunwell Trilogy, the Warcraft manga written by Richard and illustrated by Kim Jae-Hwan, and check out his new Diablo novel "Moon of the Spider" and the upcoming Sin War Trilogy. If you are in New York this weekend, make sure to check out the Tokyopop booth (booth number 415) at the NY Comic-Con to learn more about his new Warcraft novels or if you just want to grab an autograph of Richard and shake hands with the man himself!

Character Creation Management System - Eyonix on 2/15/06

We've implemented a new system that will disable character creation when a realm has a queue to better control realm populations. This system will disable character creation for accounts without characters on an individual realm when it has a queue. This will limit population growth on congested realms during peak hours and assist us in our efforts to provide a better overall gameplay experience for our players, especially those on high population realms.

Please note that players with existing characters on a queued realm will still be able to create characters, and during off hours when there is not a queue all players may create characters as normal.

Video Games Live Concert - Nebu on 2/06/06

The concert will feature over 20 games, including our very own World of Warcraft, which will feature a special video edit done by the World of Warcraft team themselves! This is the same concert that debuted in 2005 to great acclaim at the Hollywood Bowl to over 11,000 screaming video game fans in Los Angeles.
