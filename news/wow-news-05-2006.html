Shadow of the Necropolis Public Test Realms Live! - Ordinn on 5/19/06

The public test realms are live! Players can now try out all the changes and additions coming in World of Warcraft's latest content patch: Shadow of the Necropolis. The highlights of this patch include Naxxramas, a new world event, mage and shaman talent improvements, and a host of other exciting features. Read the Shadow of the Necropolis test realm patch notes to learn more.

Players who had an active account as of 12:01 AM PDT Tuesday, May 16 can participate in the test realms by creating a character, using a pre-made level 60 character, or copying their characters from existing realms to the test realms. To copy a character, visit our Public Test Realm Page. Pre-made character copies are available anytime during the testing period while personal character transfers to the test realm are available from 3:00 AM to 3:00 PM PST Monday through Friday. The test realm game client, which can be downloaded at the same page, is available for both PC and Mac.

Please be aware that the test realms are designed to allow us to test major content patches before they go public. Therefore, some changes may be introduced to the public test realms during the testing phase, before a patch goes live, and some changes may be introduced to the patch after the test realms have closed. To read more about the test realms, please review the Test Realm FAQ. We truly appreciate our players taking the time to help us test the latest changes to the game; thank you all!

Epidemiology of the Undead Plague - Nebu on 5/5/06

The plague of undeath, a product of the Lich King's morbid experiments, quickly made its presence felt after it was unleashed upon Azeroth. Its first victims were humans from a village that was unluckily situated near Icecrown Glacier. As the horrifying plague spread, it converted humans into undeath, but left the other denizens of Azeroth alive, though diseased and decaying. Was this an unintended aspect of Ner'zhul's diabolical plan, or was humanity the plague's only intended target? Read the latest page of our World of Warcraft History Library.

New Customer Service Forum Open! - Frost on 5/3/06

We are happy to announce the new Customer Service Forum! This forum is managed by Game Master department staff who assist players every day with various in-game issues via the petition system. This new forum will help players

  • Identify common in-game issues and provide solutions where available
  • Inform players of the most recent issues and give status updates as we work to resolve them
  • Grant insight into the functionality of the Game Master department to provide our players with more transparency of how we operate
  • Provide a regularly updated and comprehensive list of the most common issues at any given time, based on the reports we receive in-game
The goal of the new forum is to broaden the lines of communication between the Game Master department and the rest of the World of Warcraft community, so that issues new and old alike may be addressed accordingly. Head over to the Customer Service Forum now and check out this latest addition to the official World of Warcraft forums.
