  • Patch 2.2.0 is Live

    Posted by: Nethaera| 9-25-07
  • 9-25-07
  • The World of Warcraft team is happy to announce the release of the new 2.2.0 content patch. Highlights of Patch 2.2.0:
    • Voice Chat: The new Voice Chat feature is now available. Players will need to go into the Options menu under Sound & Voice to activate it. The new feature will be activated on the various realms in stages. For the Voice Chat launch schedule please visit the forums.
    • Mac Movie Capture: The Mac client can now capture in-game video to QuickTime movie files using a configurable choice of codec, resolution, and frame rate.
    For more information, check out the full patch notes.

  • Armory Update: Arena Calculator

    Posted by: Sun| 9-25-07
  • 9-25-07

  • We have added two updates to the World of Warcraft Armory: the Arena Calculator and an Armory browser search plugin.

    Arena Calculator

    Three Different Arena CalculatorsColor-coded FieldsReference Sheet

    The Arena Calculator is a new tool that allows you to do a number of helpful calculations for when and how you can spend your arena points. Choose one of the three useful calculators to help you answer the following questions:

    • How many arena points will I earn this week?
    • What's the average rating I'll need to get my desired arena reward?
    • When can I expect to get my desired arena reward?

    Read more about the Arena Calculator here, or try it out here.

    Browser Search Plugin

    Searching the Armory just got even more convenient!

    With the click of a button, add the World of Warcraft Armory search field to your browser for instant Armory access from anywhere on the web! Currently supported browsers are Firefox, Internet Explorer 7, and Opera. Add Armory Search to your browser today!

  • Mike Morhaime Keynotes Austin GDC

    Posted by: Nebu| 9-12-07
  • 9-12-07
  • This year's Austin Game Developer's Conference kicked off with a keynote speech from Blizzard president and cofounder Mike Morhaime. Entitled "How to Rule the World (of Warcraft) – 10 Lessons," it was a condensed version of the wisdom accumulated throughout Blizzard's history since its founding, particularly the launch of World of Warcraft and the rapid growth it necessitated. Highlights include prioritizing gameplay above all else, building and protecting the company brand, and always taking the time to finish the game before shipping it. Morhaime also gave some interviews in the aftermath of the speech, covering a wide range of topics.

  • South Park's Warcraft Episode Wins Emmy

    Posted by: Nebu| 9-10-07
  • 9-10-07
  • Congrats to the South Park team on their well-deserved Emmy win! 'Make Love, Not Warcraft,' the episode featuring the characters wielding the Sword of a Thousand Truths to defeat a menace inside World of Warcraft, picked up the Emmy for Outstanding Animated Program (For Programming Less Than One Hour).
