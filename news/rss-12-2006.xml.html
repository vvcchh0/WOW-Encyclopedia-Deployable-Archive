  • World of Warcraft Accounts Closed Worldwide

    Posted by: Tyren| 12-21-06
  • 12-21-06
  • In our continued efforts to combat cheating in World of Warcraft, more than 105,000 accounts were closed and over 12 million gold was removed from the game economies in Europe, Korea, and the US in the month of November. The closed accounts were associated with activities that violate World of Warcraft's Terms of Use, such as using third-party programs that allow cheating, and farming gold and items. These types of activities can severely impact the economy of a realm and the overall game enjoyment for all players.

    We will continue to aggressively monitor all World of Warcraft realms in order to protect the service and its players from the harmful effects of cheating. Please note that selling World of Warcraft content, such as gold, items, and characters, can result in a permanent ban of the involved accounts from World of Warcraft.

    Many account closures come as the direct result of tips reported to our GMs in game or emailed to our Hacks Team by legitimate World of Warcraft players. If you suspect that a World of Warcraft player is using an illegal third-party program to farm gold or items, or is otherwise violating our Terms of Use, please report the suspected infraction via one of the means listed above. All reports will be investigated, and those that prove false will not result in corrective action.

    As always, thank you for your continued support, and best of luck with your continued adventures in Azeroth!

  • The Burning Crusade Coming to Region of Taiwan

    Posted by: Block| 12-14-06
  • 12-14-06
  • Blizzard is pleased to announce that it has signed an agreement with Soft-World International Corporation for the operation of World of Warcraft: The Burning Crusade in the regions of Taiwan, Hong Kong, and Macau. Soft-World, Blizzard's current partner for World of Warcraft in these regions, will manage the distribution and operation of the expansion there, in addition to providing community and customer support. Localization into Traditional Chinese is underway, and both companies are working hard to complete the process and release the expansion as soon as possible following The Burning Crusade's January launch in Europe, Korea, North America, and the Oceanic regions.

    Regarding the release of World of Warcraft: The Burning Crusade in mainland China, Blizzard is continuing discussions with The9 on that topic and hopes to announce further details in the near future.

  • Player-vs.-Player Content Updated

    Posted by: Nebu| 12-05-06
  • 12-05-06
  • The new patch, "Before the Storm," implements many changes to the game, particularly to player-vs.-player combat and the Honor System as a whole. With the new Honor System, players will be able to earn honor points for spending on various rewards, from armor and equipment to potions and ammo. Because of this, ranking by honor points no longer occurs, and we have removed the PvP rankings page. You can read more about the "Before the Storm" Honor System changes here.

    In addition to the new Honor System, we have introduced the new Arena System in "Before the Storm." The new content update gives players a taste of this gladiatorial combat in non-rated matches, but The Burning Crusade will also include a new PvP ladder based on the Arena System. At level 70, players will be able to create and participate in rated matches, which allow them to earn special titles, equipment, and more. This new ranking system will be displayed on the website once teams begin challenging each other in rated arena games and start gaining arena points. Will you be the greatest gladiator in Azeroth? Play The Burning Crusade this January to find out!
