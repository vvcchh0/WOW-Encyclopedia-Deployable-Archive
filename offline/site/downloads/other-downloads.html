World of Warcraft Game Client
If you're installing The World of Warcraft on a new machine, upgrading to the Burning Crusade expansion, or are returning to the game, you can download the game client and expansion upgrades by visiting this page.

Please note:
In order to download the game client, a World of Warcraft account is required. To create a new trial account visit Warcraft.com. To upgrade to a full World of Warcraft or Burning Crusade account click here to visit Account Management.

Lords Of The Dance
Preview some of the many different dance animations in World of Warcraft and even download animated images of your favorite dances. Simply select a race and gender from the drop-down menus and watch them get into the groove. To download an animation, simply click on one of the links below the animation window and save the files to your computer. Time to start practicing your moves! Get your groove on here.

Skin for Windows Media Player
Microsoft Games for Windows has released a new skin for Windows Media Player 10 that will help bring out your inner warrior, or rogue, or hunter, or mage, or druid...while watching videos or listening to your favorite songs online (such as one of the many World of Warcraft MP3s we've posted on the WoW). Grab the new World of Warcraft Windows Media Player skin here.

World of Warcraft Desktop Theme
Now you can enjoy the look and feel of World of Warcraft even when log out of the game, thanks to the specially designed World of Warcraft Windows XP desktop theme pack created by Stardock. Using Stardock's Object Desktop technology, the World of Warcraft theme pack changes your title bars, Start menu, icons, push buttons, borders, and much more to mirror the user interface from the game. Download it here.
