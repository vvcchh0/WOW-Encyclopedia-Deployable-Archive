See what was added in Patch 1.7
Return to Previous Patches | Return to In Development

ur development team is devoted to providing players with the best play experience possible. In order to insure that World of Warcraft remains the top MMOG on the market, we will be making improvements and additions to the game on a regular basis. This includes opening new lands, introducing new monsters, incorporating new quests, and ultimately evolving the overall story of the World of Warcraft. This page will show you some of the features and enhancements included in this patch.

Click here to read the full Patch Notes.

New Features

We have implemented the following features in Patch 1.7
Please note that the New Features list is a sneak peek. It does not contain every addition, change, or bug fix going into the next patch.


Zul'Gurub

Hidden within the jungles of Stranglethorn, an ancient and perilous Troll city has been uncovered. Do you and your band of hardy explorers have what it takes to delve into its mysteries? There's only one way to find out! Zul'Gurub is a high-level, 20-man raid instance with 120 new rare and epic items to discover. Journey to Zul'Gurub and experience for yourself the adventures that await!

  • Over 120 new Epic and Superior items, including a new five-piece set for each class
  • Players can build up their reputation with the new Zandalar Tribe faction in order to gain a variety of rewards
  • Tons of Zul'Gurub quests available at the new base in Yojamba Isle
  • Highly scripted boss encounters unlike any you've experienced before!

Arathi Basin

Join the League of Arathor or the Forsaken Defilers as they battle for the precious resources stockpiled within the latest Battleground, Arathi Basin! Pitting 15 members of each faction against each other, the race is on to be the first to 2000 resources, capturing strategic landmarks around the Basin to increase your team's gain and cripple the enemy. With an all-new set of reputation-based rewards, there's never been a better time to join the war!


Hunter Talents
Curious about the changes to Hunter talents in the upcoming content patch? Wonder no longer! In this interactive preview of the changes, you can view the progression, read up on the new abilities and enhancements within the various trees, and experiment with new builds!


Stranglethorn Fishing Extravaganza
The Stranglethorn Fishing Extravaganza is a grand new event set along the coasts of Stranglethorn Vale. Click here to learn more.


The Dressing Room
Tired of spending time grinding for gold to buy a new piece of armor in the Auction House only to discover that, while the stats are great, the look leaves something to be desired? Some may argue that stats are the name of the game and the way your toon looks isn't anything to worry about, but why not have your cake and eat it too? With the Dressing Room feature in the upcoming patch, you can have both stats and a great look! Take a moment to learn more about this new, useful feature!
