"Most Level 25 Characters" Contest

World of Warcraft "Most Level 25 Characters" Contest

1. Eligibility. THIS IS A CONTEST OF SKILL. BLIZZARD ENTERTAINMENT, INC., ("SPONSOR") IS THE SPONSOR OF THIS CONTEST ("CONTEST"). THIS CONTEST IS OPEN ONLY TO RESIDENTS OF THE UNITED STATES, CANADA, AUSTRALIA, AND NEW ZEALAND THAT ARE OF THE AGE OF MAJORITY IN THEIR COUNTRY OF CITIZENSHIP AS OF MARCH 6, 2006 ("ENTRANTS"), WHO HAVE AN ACTIVE REGISTERED 'WORLD OF WARCRAFT' PLAYER ACCOUNT AS OF 12:01 P.M., PST, MARCH 6, 2006. IF YOU ARE NOT A RESIDENT OF THESE AREAS, ARE NOT EIGHTEEN (18) YEARS OF AGE OR OLDER, OR DO NOT HAVE AN ACTIVE, REGISTERED WORLD OF WARCRAFT ACCOUNT AS OF 12:01 P.M. PST, MARCH 6, 2006, YOU ARE NOT ELIGIBLE TO WIN A PRIZE. If you are under the legal age of majority in your state of residence (a "Minor"), one of your parents or legal guardians must submit a signed, written statement that they have read, understood and agree to these Contest rules with your entry, and that such agreement constitutes acceptance of these Contest rules on behalf of you and themselves. To be eligible to win a prize, entries must be completed and received by Sponsor in the format designated below. Directors, officers and employees of Sponsor, its parent, and any of their respective affiliate companies, subsidiaries, agents, professional advisors, advertising and promotional agencies, and immediate families of each are not eligible to win any prizes. All applicable federal, state and local laws and regulations apply. Offer void where prohibited. Limit one entry per World of Warcraft account. In the event of any dispute, entries will be deemed to have been submitted by the owner of the e-mail address from which the e-mail was sent.

Additionally, the Contest will take place on the "public test realms," and it is anticipated that each of the Designated Test Realms (as defined below) may not be available for certain 'extended' periods of time on a daily basis during the Contest.

3. Contest Period. The Contest shall commence on March 7, 2006 at 12:01 AM Pacific Time ("PST"), and end on March 27, 2006 at 11:59 PM PST ("Contest Period"). However, you must have a valid, registered World of Warcraft Account as of 12:01 P.M., PST, March 6, 2006 to be eligible to participate in the Contest.

4. Prizes.

A. Grand Prizes: One thousand (1,000) qualified entrants ("Grand Prize Winners") shall qualify for the opportunity to participate in a "beta test" of an upcoming Blizzard Entertainment interactive product, subject to the Grand Prize Winner agreeing to the terms of a Beta Test Agreement to receive the Grand Prize. It is expected that the Beta Test to take place prior to December 31, 2006.
5. How to Enter. The contest will take place on "World of Warcraft" internet servers that will be designated as "public test realms" specifically for the Contest by Sponsor (the "Designated Test Realms"). To participate in the Contest, a qualified entrant must create World of Warcraft characters on the Designated Test Realms, and be one of the 1,000 qualified entrants to who level the most characters to level 25 during the Contest Period. All game play must adhere to the World of Warcraft Terms of Use, and any violation thereof will invalidate your entry in the Contest. Additionally, false or deceptive entries or acts, as well as acts of cheating, will render the entrant ineligible.

ALL FEDERAL, STATE AND LOCAL TAXES ASSOCIATED WITH THE RECEIPT OR USE OF ANY PRIZES ARE THE SOLE RESPONSIBILITY OF THE WINNER. All prizes will be awarded. Prizes returned as undeliverable or otherwise not claimed within fifteen (15) days after delivery of notification will be forfeited and awarded to an alternate winner. Prizes are not transferable. No substitutions or exchanges (including for cash) of any prizes will be permitted, except that Sponsor reserves the right to substitute a prize of equal or greater value for any prize. Limit one prize per household or address. All prizes are awarded "AS IS" and WITHOUT WARRANTY OF ANY KIND, express or implied, (including, without limitation, any implied warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose)

6. Determination of Winners. The one thousand who level the most characters to level 25 during the Contest Period will be chosen as Grand Prize Winners.

7. Collection of Information. The information collected to enter the Contest will be used to contact you in the event that you are chosen to receive a prize. Additionally, Sponsor will use this information for marketing purposes, and to contact you regarding offers, promotions and other Contests related to this and/or other products distributed by Sponsor. As stated above, the Contest is not open to individuals who are not of the age of majority in their countries of citizenship by March 6, 2006, and in the event that Sponsor learns that you are not of the age of majority in your country of citizenship, and you submit an entry to Sponsor, Sponsor will delete, erase or destroy any and all personally identifiable information that is obtained from the entry.
