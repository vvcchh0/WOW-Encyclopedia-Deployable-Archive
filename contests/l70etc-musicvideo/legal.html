    • Three (3) Grand Prize winners will receive one (1) Ipod Touch 8GB version, with an approximate retail value of $299.00.

  5. How to Enter.

    Submit your World of Warcraft music video utilizing only music provided by Level 70 Elite Tauren Chieftain via the "Level 70 Elite Tauren Chieftain Music Video" contest submission page (the "Music") located at http://www.worldofwarcraft.com/contests/l70etc-musicvideo/submit.html. Videos can be no larger than 50 megabytes in file size and must be submitted in WMV, MOV, or AVI formats by May 19, 2008 at 12:01 AM PT. All submissions exceeding said limit, and/or including music other than the Music, and/or including any materials which are not entrant's own original work, or encoded in an alternate format will be void. You must fill out the following fields in order to be eligible: First Name, Address 1, Address 2 (if applicable), City, State, Zip/Postal Code, World of Warcraft Account Name (if applicable), and Email.

    All footage used in entries must be entirely your own original creation and unreleased at the time of contest submission.

  6. Determination of Winners.

    Qualified entrants for this Contest may submit videos that depict character(s) from World of Warcraft. The entries shall be judges by a panel of five (5) Judges selected by Sponsor. Sponsor and its Judges, based on their sole discretion, shall judge the videos based equally on composition, creativity, and originality. All decisions of Sponsor and its Judges are final. Sponsor shall choose the winners by May 30, 2008 from all qualified entrants. Prize winners will be notified by e-mail within fourteen (14) days thereafter. To claim a prize, winners must follow the instructions contained in their notification, which may require that Winner provide Sponsor a version of their winning entry in an uncompressed format on a Data DVD. Entrants can only win once during the Contest Period.
