World of Warcraft Community Timeline
November, 2004
  • World of Warcraft launches in North America, Australia and New Zealand.
  • "I Surrender" debuts

    December, 2004

  • Maraudon
  • Feast of Winter Veil

    January, 2005

  • Captain Placeholder is born
  • World of Warcraft launches in Korea

    February, 2005

  • World of Warcraft launches in Europe

    March, 2005
  • Public Test Realms go live
  • Dire Maul
  • World of Warcraft appears on Pure Pwnage with "Wow Is a Feeling"
  • Outdoor Raid Bosses appear - Kazzak/Azuregos
  • World of Warcraft sets a new milestone with 1.5 million subscribers worldwide

    April, 2005
  • "The Murloc is lonely" Fan Fiction begins
  • Honor System Implemented
  • Captain Placeholder leaves Azeroth

    May, 2005

  • Leeroy Jenkins
  • Battleground played for the first time at E3

    June, 2005

  • World of Warcraft launches in China
  • Release of Battlegrounds
  • World of Warcraft achieves new milestone with 2 million subscribers worldwide
  • "Rise of the Living Dead" episode 1 debuts

    July, 2005
  • Blackwing Lair
  • Darkmoon Faire
  • Captain Jean-Luc Picard of the USS Enterprise (Picard-a-thon 2005)
  • World of Warcraft launches in Singapore

    August, 2005

  • Shadow Priests begin melting faces in PvP
  • World of Warcraft surpasses 1 million customer milestone in North America

    September, 2005
  • Zul'Gurub
  • Arathi Basin
  • RP-PvP Realms launched
  • Corrupted Blood Plague sweeps Azeroth!
  • "Return"

    October, 2005
  • World of Warcraft reaches a new milestone with a worldwide subscriber base of over 4.5 million players!
  • The Stirring of the Silithid
  • Four Dragons
  • BlizzCon
  • World of Warcraft: The Burning Crusade announced

    November, 2005
  • World of Warcraft launches in Taiwan, Hong Kong and Macau
  • World of Warcraft appears on Jeopardy
