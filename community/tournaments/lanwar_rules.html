Official Rules
LANWAR Blizzard Tournaments

Please read these rules before entering your name for the " Lanwar Blizzard Tournaments " to be held on June 15, 2006 through June 18, 2006 at "Lanwar." (collectively referred to herein as the "Contest"). By entering and participating in the Contest, you agree to be bound by these Official Rules and represent that you satisfy all of the eligibility requirements in paragraph 1 below.

1. Eligibility. BLIZZARD ENTERTAINMENT, INC., (REFERED TO HEREIN AS "BLIZZARD"); IS THE SPONSOR OF THIS CONTEST. THIS CONTEST IS OPEN ONLY TO INDIVIDUALS WHO ARE OF THE AGE OF MAJORITY IN THEIR COUNTRY OF RESIDENCE WHO ATTEND LANWAR ("ENTRANTS"). If you are under the legal age of majority in your country of residence (a "Minor"), one of your parents or legal guardians must read and agree to these Contest rules on behalf of you and themselves in order to win a prize. To be eligible to win a prize, entries must be completed and received by Blizzard in the format designated below. Directors, officers and employees of Blizzard, its parent, and any of their respective affiliate companies, subsidiaries, agents, professional advisors, advertising and promotional agencies, and immediate families of each are not eligible to win any prizes. All applicable federal, state and local laws and regulations apply. Offer void where prohibited. Participants must have a Battle.net account which includes an associated valid email address. Blizzard reserves the right to verify the email address provided to Blizzard by Entrant. In the event of any dispute, entries containing an invalid email address will be deemed ineligible.

2. Disclaimer. Blizzard, Prize Suppliers, and any of their respective parent companies, subsidiaries, affiliates, directors, officers, professional advisors, employees and agencies will not be responsible for (a) any late, lost, misrouted, garbled or distorted or damaged transmissions or entries; (b) telephone, electronic, hardware, software, network, Internet, or other computer- or communications-related malfunctions or failures; (c) any Contest disruptions, injuries, losses or damages caused by events beyond the control of Blizzard; or (d) any printing or typographical errors in any materials associated with the Contest.

3. Contest Period. The Contest shall commence on June 15, 2006 at 8 a.m. Eastern Standard Ti Time (EST), and shall continue in duration through June 18, 2006 at 8:00 p.m. EST (the "Deadline").

4. How to Enter. The Contest is a contest of skill. To enter one of the Contests, you can signup at Lanwar.com or at the Blizzard booth at Lanwar on June 15, 2006 through June 18, 2006 until all tournament spots have been filled, or the last "qualifying round" for each of the separate Battle.net Tournaments begins.

5. General Rules regarding the Lanwar Blizzard Tournament Play. Any disconnection between match players due to System, Network, PC, and/or Power problems/issues shall be judged according to the following guidelines: (i) In the event that Blizzard determines that a player has intentionally Disconnected to stop a match, the offending player will be charged with a loss by forfeit; (ii) in the case of an unintentional disconnection that occurs within 5 minutes of start of match, Blizzard will restart the match; and (iii) in the case of an unintentional disconnection that occurs 5 minutes after the start of the match:

A. If the players cannot reach an agreement, the winner will be determined by the Blizzard after the analysis of the match replay file.

B. If the winner can not be decided by analyzing the match replay file, the players must agree to a match restart or forfeit the match.
The intentional use of a program bugs will result in a warning at the minimum or loss by default for the offending player, in Blizzard's sole discretion. Also, Blizzard will does not allow "Unfair Play." "Unfair Play" is defined as: (i) Use of any map hack program; (ii) An intentional disconnection; (iii) the use of any settings exceeding standard and permitted settings; and (iv) during the course of any match, Blizzard may determine other actions to be unfair play at any time. Upon discovery of any player committing any violations regarded as unfair play, that player will be disqualified from the tournament. Two warnings constitute being disqualified from the tournament.

6. Specific Settings for Each of the Lanwar Blizzard Tournaments. The settings for each of the Lanwar Blizzard Tournaments shall be the following:

  • A. Game Version: WarCraft III: Frozen Throne v1.20d
    1. General
      • Competition Method: 2 vs. 2
      • Match Length: Until the winner is determined
      • Match Winner: The player who completely destroys the opponent or if the opponent surrenders
    2. Game Settings
      • Race Selection: Free Choice
      • Game Speed: Fastest
      • Maps: Lost Temple(Region of Chaos), Turtle Rock, Terenas Stand, Twisted Meadows, Gnoll Wood (The Frozen Throne)
      • The map selection may be changed prior to the tournament. Players will be informed of any such changes before the tournament.
    3. Tournament Server Settings
      • Lock Teams: Yes
      • Teams Together: No
      • Full shared unit control: No
      • Full shared unit control: No
      • Random races: No
      • Random hero: No
      • Observers: Full Observers
      • Visibility: Default
  • B. Game Version: StarCraft: Brood War v1.13
    1. General
      • Competition Method: 1 vs. 1
      • Match Length: Until the winner is determined
      • Match Winner: The player who completely destroys the opponent or if the opponent surrenders
    2. Game Settings
      • Race Selection: Free Choice
      • Game Speed: Fastest
      • Maps: Lost Temple 2.4, Gaia, Nightlights 1.2, Signal, Luna the Final
      • The map selection may be changed prior to the tournament. Players will be informed of any such changes before the tournament.
  • C. Game Version: WarCraft III: DotA v 6.32b
    1. General
      • Competition Method: 5 vs. 5
      • Match Length: Until the winner is determined
      • Match Winner: The players who completely destroys the opponents tree or if the opponent surrenders
    2. Game Settings
      • Race Selection: League Mode
      • Game Speed: Normal
      • Maps: DotA 6.32b
    3. Tournament Server Settings
      • Lock Teams: Yes
      • Full shared unit control: No
      • Full shared unit control: No
      • Random hero: No
      • Visibility: Default
  • D. Game Version: World of Warcraft 1.11
    1. Leveling Contest
      • Each participant will receive one (1) blank account to create one (1) new character
      • Race restrictions: None
      • Class restrictions: None
    2. Warsong Gulth: Meatgrinder
      • Each participant will receive one (1) account with 9 template characters to choose from
      • Class restrictions per team: None
    3. Stratholme Race
      • Each participant will receive one (1) account with 9 template characters to choose from
      • Class restrictions per team: None
    4. User Interface (UI) Mods allowed
      • The most current list of allowed UI mods will be available at the Blizzard booth at Lanwar June 15, 200 through June 18, 2006

7. Prizes. The top four (4) entrants who are adjudged by Blizzard to be the winners of the Starcraft 1v1 Tournament will win a Razer™ Copperhead™ Mouse with an approximate retail value of $58.99 USD. Each member of the two (2) teams that win the Warcraft III 2v2 tournament and each member of the winning team of the Warcraft III: Defense of the Ancients tournament will receive a "Grom Hellscream" Resin Statue with an approximate retail value of $100.00 USD. The top ten (10) finishing participants in the World of Warcraft: Fast Leveling Contest will receive a Sound Blaster X-Fi Fatal1ty™ sound card with an approximate retail value of $293.00 USD. The second top ten (10) participants (places 11-20) will receive a Fatal1ty 1010 Mouse™ with an approximate retail value of $49.99 USD. The team who completes the World of Warcraft: Stratholme Race in the least amount of time will each receive a Zalman Fatal1ty™ Champion custom computer with an approximate retail value of $3,000 USD.
