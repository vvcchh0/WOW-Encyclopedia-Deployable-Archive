Blizzard European Regionals 2007
When:
June 22nd to 24th 2007
Where:
Hamburg, Germany - CCH
What:
World of Warcraft Arena - Season 1 European Finals
Warcraft III - Battle.net Season 5 European Finals

The Season of Battle is almost over and the final competitions have started. After the Regionals in Asia at the Worldwide Invitational and the American Regionals in San Diego, the last of the Regionals is about to start. The eight best European World of Warcraft Arena Teams and the eight best European Warcraft III players have been invited to compete for the honor of fighting for the World Championship at BlizzCon 2007 against the world elite. These will be the first European World of Warcraft Arena Regionals to be held and we are especially proud of this new addition to our tournament schedule. We want to congratulate all these players on their great achievement of reaching the Regional Finals this year and wish them good luck for the competition.

Arena Tournament - Season 1 - European Regional Finals

The first European Regional Finals of the Arena Tournament will be fought out in double elimination rounds with best out of five games for each encounter. The matches take place in the three World of Warcraft Arenas "The Ring of Trials", "The Circle of Blood" and the new addition "Lordaeron".
The teams competing in these Regionals were the best teams in their battlegroup in Season 1 and were then invited to compete in qualifiers where they played against the other qualifying teams from that region's battlegroups. At the end of the qualifier round the top 8 teams from each region were chosen to participate in live regional events. More information on the tournament can be found on the Arena Tournament Preview page, the Armory and in the Arena Tournament FAQ. Warcraft III - Battle.net Season 5 - European Regional Finals

While the Arena Tournament is brand new this year the Battle.net tournament has a history of exciting matches and great players who have shown amazing skill over the past years. And once again, for the fifth time, eight great players have proven themselves worthy of competing for the title of European Champion and the chance to attend the Global Finals to be held at BlizzCon on August 3rd and 4th this year.

The tournament will be a Solo-Player in double elimination rounds with best out of three games for each match. Though only two of the six European players that were in the Global finals last season have made it into the Regionals, there are many well-known names in the list. The winner of this year's Worldwide Invitational, ToD, will be there and young talent, XlorD, will give us an impression of what the professional players of tomorrow have in store for us. American Regional Finals - San Diego

The World of Warcraft and Warcraft III American regional finals were held at the House of Blues on June 2nd and 3rd in San Diego, California. The best American Warcraft III players and the best World of Warcraft Arena teams from the American and Oceanic region competed for the title of American Champion and the ticket to the Global Finals at the BlizzCon.

Shoutcaster Kim "bunny" Phan led the audience through the Warcraft III matches while World of Warcraft celebrity Ben "Leeroy Jenkins" Shoals and Jason "NiceguyEd" Hutchison commented on the fast-paced Arena matches that were broadcasted by Epileptic Gaming on their live stream.

The final games of the American World of Warcraft and Warcraft III regionals concluded with some tense and exciting matches. The winners in ranking order are:

Asian Regional Finals - Worldwide Invitational 2007

The Blizzard Worldwide Invitational is an event that allows the company to connect with gamers around the world in a celebration of the Warcraft, Diablo, and StarCraft game universes and competitive Blizzard Entertainment gaming. This year's event took place in Seoul, South Korea on May 19th and 20th and featured a number of e-sports tournaments, including the regional finals for both Warcraft III Season 5 and the first season of World of Warcraft's Arena Tournament. Invitational StarCraft and Warcraft III tournaments also pitted some of Korea's finest players against tough international challengers.

World of Warcraft Arena Tournament: Notorious Dominates
The World of Warcraft Arena regional finals in Korea have been very impressive. Moreover, the last rounds of the competition have been particularly exciting. During the upper bracket finals, D E R Tenacity faced Notorious. In the end, the Notorious team proved its superiority, winning first place. The top three teams were:

    1. Notorious
    2. DER Tenacity
    3. Myth

All of these teams will participate in the global finals and will go on to face the top players around the world.
