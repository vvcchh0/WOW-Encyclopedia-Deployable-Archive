orld of Warcraft is alive with activity from members of
both the Horde and Alliance. Each day they face
deadly threats and overcome them with the help of
others, building lasting personal connections in the process.
Through the years, we have heard inspirational stories of friendships found, friendships rediscovered, romances kindled, and even entire guilds rallying behind a member to give support in a time of need. These stories have touched many people and helped make the players on the other side of the keyboard more than just names in a chat box.

We would like to invite those with inspirational personal stories to share them for consideration in our new series. We want to hear about the positive impact World of Warcraft has had on you and your life. Your story could be chosen to be featured on our website.

For rules, privacy, and submission requirements,
please visit our Story Submission page.
