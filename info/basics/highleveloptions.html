PvP

Player vs. Player Combat

You have equipped yourself and are at the maximum level. Now it's time to use your experience to defeat other players. Discover Player vs. Player combat! There are various events you can compete in:
  • Honor System - Earn honor points to use to buy mounts, items, and more
  • The Arena - Compete on a ladder and earn very powerful items
  • Four Battlegrounds - Earn special items and honor points
  • Azeroth Outdoor Pvp
  • Outland Outdoor Pvp - Some rewards can be gained here
  • Gurubashi Arena Booty Run
  • Fight at a special arena in Dire Maul

Player vs. Player Rewards

While participating in Player vs. Player combat you can earn Honor Points to spend on items. To find the rewards Alliance players should head to Champions Hall in Old Town in Stormwind. Horde players go to the Hall of Legends in Orgrimmar. Inside you'll find NPCs with cool things to buy. For the Battleground rewards you'll have to head to the entrances of the Battlegrounds to purchase some of the rewards (Warsong Gulch/Arathi Basin). Professions Mastering
If you wish to be a master craftsman, you will need to work hard and spend time perfecting your skills. Certain special recipes can only be collected in high level dungeons; the bosses within can sometimes hold plans for very powerful items. Once you have these recipes you will be able to create new items that you can use yourself, give to friends, help your guild, or sell to other players for a king's ransom.

Other

Tried an "Alt"?

Now that you've powered up one character up to level 70, perhaps it's time to try out another class! It's much easier the next time around, as you now know where you're going and where to find quests and adventure. You can also spend some money to equip your low level character with powerful weapons and equipment to give yourself an edge. If you have played the Horde, you might try an Alliance character or vice versa.

Tried a Blood Elf or Draenei?

You can level up in completely new areas in The Burning Crusade expansion. Start a Blood Elf or Draenei character.
