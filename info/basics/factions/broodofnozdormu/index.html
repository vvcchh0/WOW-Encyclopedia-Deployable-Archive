Brood of Nozdormu
n the lives of most mortals, time is nothing more than a simple hourglass, its sands quietly trickling away. Only few ever realize that in truth, time rages like a storm, untamed and chaotic. When the Titans first descended on Azeroth, vanquishing the Old Gods and bringing order to the world, the Highfather of the Pantheon himself empowered the great dragon Nozdormu with some of his own cosmic powers, enabling the dragon to guard time itself and police the ever-spinning pathways of fate and destiny. And so, the stoic, honorable Nozdormu became known as the Timeless One. Ever since then, Nozdormu's Brood has guarded the timestream from all who would seek to disrupt its flow, guided by the Timeless One's wise, all-knowing counsel.

Now, the bronze dragon Anachronos has returned outside the Caverns of Time in the Tanaris desert, the Bronze Dragonflight's home and province. Events have been set in motion that will shape the future of all of Azeroth, as the attention of the Brood of Nozdormu is focused on Ahn'Qiraj, the fortress city of the Qiraji. It is there that those who are brave enough to heed the dragon's call should travel. A terrible darkness is building up beyond the Scarab Wall, enough to give pause even to the Timeless One himself. The dragons are rallying their champions to descend into the nightmarish hives beneath Ahn'Qiraj and to finish what they have begun.

The fated hour draws near...

Tips
Here are some tips on gaining reputation with the Brood of Nozdormu. In order to unlock some of the rewards, the gates of Ahn'Qiraj must be open on your realm.

Starting off
You begin with hated reputation after completing the quest What Tomorrow Brings. This quest begins with Baristolth in Cenarion Hold. From that point, your goal is to increase your reputation in order to unlock the rewards listed above.

The Opening of the Gates of Ahn'Qiraj
If your realm has not yet opened the Gates of Ahn'Qiraj, there is a chance to earn some reputation during the opening event. For ten hours following the opening of the gates, Ahn'Qiraj monsters appear that can be killed for up to 500 reputation points. You can read more about that here. After the event is over, this option is no longer available. If you get a chance to open the gates, show up for some possible reputation gains.

Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj (AQ20)
You can gain Brood of Nozdormu reputation in the Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj (AQ20) by killing the bosses. The gains in this instance are only minor with 33 reputation gained per boss.

The Hand of the Righteous
After the player has completed the quest "Only One May Rise", they may undergo the quest, "The Hand of the Righteous." After turning in 200 Silithid Carapace Fragments, they may deputize another player to gather these fragments. Every time the player turns in 200 Fragments they receive 500 reputation, and is allowed to deputize another player. This quest can be completed repeatedly until the player is Neutral with the Brood of Nozdormu.

Temple of Ahn'Qiraj (AQ40)
The majority of reputation with Brood of Nozdormu will probably be gained in this dungeon. If you continue to raid this dungeon on a regular basis and kill bosses you will build up your reputation. Most monsters will give 100 reputation per kill up until you are 2999/3000 Neutral. After that point, the only monsters that will give reputation are the final bosses of the dungeon: Twin Emperors and C'Thun.

Each boss in AQ40 drops a Qiraji Lord's Insignia. These can be turned in at an NPC after defeating the Prophet Skeram within the dungeon for 500 reputation with Brood of Nozdormu and 100 Cenarion Circle reputation. You should hold on to these until you have reached 2999/3000 Neutral where you no longer gain reputation for most monster kills. After you reach that point, turn them all in at once.

Monsters can also drop Ancient Qiraji Artifacts which can be turned in for 1000 reputation. As with the Insignias, you should save these until you have reached the end of Neutral (2999/3000). These are somewhat rare.
