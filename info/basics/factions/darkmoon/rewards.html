Darkmoon Faire Rewards
t the Darkmoon Faire, there are two ways to get rewards: collecting cards to create "decks" and turning in Darkmoon tickets. Read below to find out more!

Deck Rewards
isted below are items that are rewarded when you turn in different decks. Decks are created by collecting cards which can be looted from various creatures around the world. Once you have all the cards of a specific deck, you combine them to create a deck. The first four decks drop in Azeroth while the next four drop in Outland.

isted below are items that are rewarded when you turn in various amounts of tickets. Tickets can be obtained by turning in various items to NPCs at the Darkmoon Faire.

Step right up, step right up! I'm Gelvas Grimegate and if you've got Darkmoon tickets, then I've got prizes! It doesn't matter if you've got five tickets or if you've got twelve hundred tickets! Give me your tickets and I'll give you fantastic prizes - prizes that you can't find anywhere else in all of Azeroth, guaranteed! Don't be shy, step right up and turn in those tickets!
